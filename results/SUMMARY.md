## Takeaways

- **Convolutional autoencoder:** Learned from 188 defect-free images only (0 defect images in training; threshold set on the 41 labelled split-A images). AUROC 0.928; at its split-A threshold it rejected 28/32 defective and 2/10 good bottles; weakest defect type: contamination (64% caught).
- **ResNet-18 classifier:** Trained with 31 labelled defect images. AUROC 0.988; at threshold 0.5 it rejected 31/32 defective and 0/10 good bottles; weakest defect type: contamination (91% caught).
- **FoundAD (DINOv2 few-shot):** Learned from only 4 defect-free images on top of a frozen DINOv2 encoder (0 defect images in training; threshold set on the 41 labelled split-A images). AUROC 1.000; at its split-A threshold it rejected 32/32 defective and 0/10 good bottles; caught 100% of every defect type.
